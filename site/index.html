Three towns · one ZIP

Where 15068 draws its line

The ZIP code takes in three cities: New Kensington's grid on the river flat, Arnold just upriver, and Lower Burrell on the hills above. It also clips small edges of Upper Burrell, Plum and Allegheny Township.

Sources & method

The map data is pulled straight from open datasets and clipped to the Census boundary for ZIP 15068: Overture Maps for buildings, roads, places and addresses, and AWS Terrain Tiles for elevation. Nothing on the map is hand-placed. The history, civic and news sections were compiled from published sources, and each entry links to its source. Business listings can go stale, so call ahead.
