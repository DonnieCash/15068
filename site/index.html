      Three cities share one ZIP code

      New Kensington holds the river-flat street grid, Arnold sits just upriver, and Lower Burrell covers the hills above. The ZIP also clips edges of Upper Burrell, Plum and Allegheny Township.

      Crime and policing in the three cities

      What each police department reports to the FBI, who the departments are, and incidents local news reported at a place you can find on the map.

      Note on these numbers. Rates are per 1,000 residents. Small towns swing from year to year, and a few incidents can move a rate a lot. The mapped incidents are a news-reported sample, not every call. No suspects, victims or line officers are named, and locations are rounded to the block or placed on the street.

      About NK15068

      The map is drawn from open datasets clipped to the Census boundary for ZIP 15068: Overture Maps for buildings, roads, places and addresses, and AWS Terrain Tiles for elevation. Nothing on the map is placed by hand. The news, history, civic, crime and pets sections were compiled from published sources, and each entry links to where it was reported.
